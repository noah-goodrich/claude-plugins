## Findings

- Trigger description: no signal to climb. Train and heldout are both at ceiling (no miss, no false fire). The intervals are wide, so "0 of 28 false fires" only rules out a false-fire rate above about 12 percent. A hillclimb on this suite cannot show improvement; it can only detect regression. Harder near-misses are needed before the description is worth rewriting.
- Competitors: anthropic-skills `deep-research` never fired on the three "deep research" prompts (F14 to F16); research-tools:research took them. `design-doc` fired on all three design-doc near-misses (N12 to N14) and `strategic-brief` on both build-vs-buy ones (N15, N16), correctly. `cortex-router` never fired.
- Mode declaration: the skill does not reliably state its mode. 4 of 6 runs never named a mode in their text, and M3 went straight to a recommendation. M5 (hybrid expected) declared evidence. The pre-registered check counts undeclared as incorrect, so mode accuracy is 1/6 with a wide interval; the cleaner reading is "declared 2 of 6, right 1 of 2". Phase 0 says to pick a mode but nothing requires printing it.
- B1 (no epub requested): PASS. analysis.md under `docs/research/2026-11-03-water-before-meal/`, first line `Generated: 2026-11-03`, no `.gate-armed`, no pandoc or epub tool call, no .epub. The one Bash call was `pwd; ls`, denied by the guard.
- B2 (epub requested): PASS-weak. The skill wrote the report, then said it could not make the epub because Bash was disabled. It made no pandoc tool call: its one earlier Bash call (a grep) was denied, so it knew Bash was off and never attempted pandoc. The signal is an explicit epub step in the transcript, not an attempted call. Counted as a pass of the weak kind because the brief allowed "an explicit epub step"; a strict reading (attempted pandoc) would be a FAIL, so this is the verdict to look at first.
- The skill did not stop to ask in either behavior run despite the pre-confirmation. Both finished in 25 turns.
- pandoc never ran and nothing was written outside the fixture (guard hook plus no Bash permission).

## Known text contradictions (reported, not fixed)

- `reading-deliverable-standard.md:46-47` says ai-scoring is presented with its score, not gated, while `:122` is a checklist item "score >= 75". Is 75 a gate?
- `research/references/research-document-template.md:148` shows a decimal credibility score (`Score: 7.8`) while `research/SKILL.md:231` says to report the band word, not a decimal.

## Caveats

- Single model, small n, wide intervals; B1 and B2 are n=1 each.
- Fire is detected from Skill tool_use only; a run capped at 3 turns that would have fired on turn 4 counts as a miss.
- Mode is read from assistant text by regex ("mode ... X", "X mode", or bold X). A model that states the mode in other words reads as undeclared.
- Rep-2 trigger runs were not stratified, so per-split intervals mix 1 and 2 reps of the same query; runs of one query are not independent.
