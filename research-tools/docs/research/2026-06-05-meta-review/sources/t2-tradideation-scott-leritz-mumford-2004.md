## Key Findings

- Based on 70 studies, "well-designed creativity training programs typically induce gains in
  performance" that generalize across criteria, settings, and populations (overall d≈0.64).
- The most effective programs "focus on development of cognitive skills and the heuristics
  involved in skill application, using realistic exercises appropriate to the domain at hand."
- Of 11 identified training clusters, idea-production and cognitive training were most effective;
  imagery training was least effective.
- Represents the pre-2024 optimistic consensus that Sio & Lortie-Forgues (2024) later corrected
  downward for publication bias — keep both to show the trajectory of the evidence.

## Inclusion Decision

**Decision:** Supporting
**Rationale:** Authority + Specificity. Establishes the prior consensus that creativity methods
work and identifies WHICH components matter (cognitive heuristics, realistic domain exercises) — a
finding that survives the 2024 deflation in direction if not magnitude.

**Redundancy check:** Partially superseded by Sio 2024 on overall magnitude, but uniquely
documents the cluster-level finding (cognitive/idea-production > imagery) and the pre-correction
baseline.
