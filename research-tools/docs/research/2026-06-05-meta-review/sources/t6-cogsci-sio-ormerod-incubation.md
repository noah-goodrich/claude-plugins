## Key Findings

- The 117-study meta-analysis found a positive but modest incubation effect (mean d = 0.29) across
  insight and divergent tasks. Real, but not large.
- Incubation benefits are LARGER when the break is filled with an UNDEMANDING task than with a
  demanding task or rest — a precise, actionable design parameter.
- Two candidate mechanisms: spreading activation (remote associations form during the gap) and
  set-shifting / forgetting of misleading fixation ("a fresh start is possible").
- Design implication: an ideation tool should build in deliberate low-load pauses between generation
  passes, not relentless continuous output.
