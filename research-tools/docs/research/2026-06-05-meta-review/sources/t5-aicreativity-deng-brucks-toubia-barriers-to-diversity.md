## Key Findings

- Two distinct mechanisms cause low LLM idea diversity: (1) FIXATION at the individual level
  (autoregressive anchoring + typicality bias from alignment) and (2) lack of KNOWLEDGE
  PARTITIONING at the collective level (LLMs sample from one unified distribution, whereas humans
  each occupy distinct regions of the knowledge space).
- Humans and LLMs fixate to a STATISTICALLY SIMILAR degree (slope β=1.035 human vs 1.013 LLM,
  p=0.51) — fixation is not uniquely an AI flaw. The collective gap is the bigger problem.
- Human first-idea diversity significantly exceeds LLM (pairwise distance 2.41 vs 1.51, p<0.001).
- Chain-of-Thought (short titles + "make them different") reduces fixation; "ordinary personas"
  (not "creative entrepreneur" personas) recover knowledge partitioning by cueing distinct
  semantic regions.
- Combining personas + CoT produces the HIGHEST diversity, "not only closing the gap but
  outperforming humans" (~26% more unique combinations: 248 vs ~197 human baseline).
