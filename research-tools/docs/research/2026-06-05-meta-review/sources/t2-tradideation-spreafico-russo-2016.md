## Key Findings

- Surveyed 200+ industrial case studies (TRIZ Journal + ETRIA TRIZ Future Conference) and conclude
  TRIZ "is one of the most effective and accepted methods" for system innovation.
- The contradiction matrix and 40 inventive principles are among the most-used tools in practice,
  but TRIZ is frequently integrated with FMEA, QFD, and Lean rather than used standalone.
- Despite claimed effectiveness, TRIZ "has never reached the level of capillarity expected" — slow
  diffusion driving the community to simplify and hybridize the method.
- Important caveat (institutional-vs-ground): the corpus is composed of self-reported success
  cases; the survey does not include failed TRIZ applications, so the effectiveness claim is built
  on a survivorship-biased sample.
