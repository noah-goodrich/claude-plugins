## Key Findings

- Identifies a "missing middle": AI academic search (Elicit, Undermind, Consensus) is poor for both
  undergraduate info-literacy and exhaustive evidence synthesis, but strong for working researchers'
  narrative literature reviews.
- These tools are tuned for high early precision and "reasonable recall," NOT exhaustive coverage — they
  reliably surface relevant work in the top 10/20/50 but do not exhaust the literature or give formal
  exhaustiveness, making them unfit for systematic reviews.
- In his own gold-standard recall test, Undermind hit recall@10 of 72.7% (8/11 relevant papers) and recall@50
  of 81.8% — strong for quick discovery, insufficient for comprehensive synthesis.
- Lived/independent corrective to vendor "superhuman" / "comprehensive" framing: the right question is fit-to-
  use-case, not a single winner.
