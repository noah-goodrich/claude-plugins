## Key Findings

- Robin orchestrates three agents — Crow, Falcon (literature/search) and Finch (data analysis) — to
  propose and pre-clinically validate novel treatments. The intellectual loop (hypotheses, experiment
  choices, data analysis, figures) is AI-generated; humans execute the physical experiments.
- Robin identified ripasudil (an approved ROCK inhibitor) as a novel candidate for dry age-related
  macular degeneration by proposing enhancement of RPE phagocytosis as the therapeutic strategy.
- The full cycle from system conception to paper submission took ~2.5 months; the announcement claims
  Robin compressed a workflow estimated at ~872-937 hours of human cognitive labor.
- This is the clearest separation of "AI as the scientist (hypotheses + analysis)" from "human as the
  hands (bench execution)" — a hybrid that sidesteps the robotic-lab bottleneck.
