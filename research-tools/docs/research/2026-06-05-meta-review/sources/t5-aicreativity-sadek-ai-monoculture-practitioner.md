## Key Findings

- The practitioner fear is a convergence toward a "bland, generic, AI-inflected mean" that drowns
  out idiosyncratic voices — the human-experienced version of the homogenization metric.
- The proposed remedy is behavioral, not technical: refuse to outsource the creative STRUGGLE;
  keep human intuition "in full rein" so collaboration yields idiosyncratic outputs.
- Warns that outsourcing structure, editing, and refinement to AI risks ATROPHYING analytical
  capability and removing the "productive friction" that generates authentic insight — a lived-
  experience echo of the CHI 2025 skill-atrophy finding.
- Frames AI as not inherently homogenizing: the output depends on whether the human keeps creative
  control or cedes it — consistent with the "it's the design/usage, not AI per se" thread.
