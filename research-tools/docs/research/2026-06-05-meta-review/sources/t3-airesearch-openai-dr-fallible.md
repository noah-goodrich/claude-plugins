## Key Findings

- Carries OpenAI's OWN admitted limitations: Deep Research "can sometimes hallucinate facts in responses or make
  incorrect inferences, though at a notably lower rate than existing ChatGPT models."
- OpenAI separately concedes it may struggle to distinguish authoritative information from rumors and is weak at
  confidence calibration (failing to convey uncertainty) — vendor-acknowledged, not just external critique.
- Independent critique: the agent summarizes but does not understand importance, missed major legal rulings and
  scientific updates, and cannot distinguish authoritative from unreliable sources.
- OpenAI Deep Research scored 26.6% on Humanity's Last Exam (state-of-the-art at launch), framing it as strong
  on benchmarks yet still requiring human oversight.
- Core limit: "can't question its own assumptions, highlight knowledge gaps, think creatively or understand
  different perspectives" — the shallow-synthesis ceiling.
