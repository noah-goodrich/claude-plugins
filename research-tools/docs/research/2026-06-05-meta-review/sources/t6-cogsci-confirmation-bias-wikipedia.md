## Key Findings

- Confirmation bias = "the tendency to search for, interpret, favor and recall information in a way that
  confirms or supports one's prior beliefs." Wason coined the term from his rule-discovery (2-4-6) task.
- Klayman & Ha (1987) reinterpreted Wason's results: people use a "positive test strategy" — a heuristic,
  not necessarily a motivated bias. This is the key CONTESTED nuance: what looks like bias may be an
  efficient default test strategy that misfires in specific environments.
- Higher cognitive ability does NOT reliably reduce myside bias (Stanovich & West). Expertise/intelligence
  is not a defense — a load-bearing point for any tool: you cannot assume the smart user self-corrects.
