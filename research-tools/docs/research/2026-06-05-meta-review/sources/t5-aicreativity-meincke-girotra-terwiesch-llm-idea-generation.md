## Key Findings

- GPT-4 generated ideas with higher AVERAGE quality (purchase intent) than human students; of the
  top 40 ideas judged best, 35 came from ChatGPT-4.
- AI ideas were rated as LESS novel and showed HIGHER pairwise similarity than human ideas,
  especially under few-shot prompting — the quality/novelty trade-off in product form.
- A companion line of work ("Prompting Diverse Ideas," Meincke/Mollick/Terwiesch) found GPT-4 idea
  pools are less diverse than groups of humans, but Chain-of-Thought prompting raises diversity
  closest to human-group levels — diversity is recoverable via prompting.
- Practical implication: AI raises the floor and the average, but a single model run is a poor
  source of breadth; diversity "needs special protection" (Terwiesch).
