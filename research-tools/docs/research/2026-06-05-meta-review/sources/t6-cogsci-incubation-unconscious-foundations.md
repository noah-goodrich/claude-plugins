## Key Findings

- Incubation = conscious work, then a period refraining from task-related conscious thought, then return
  with new insight. The associative account: unconscious processing "is more adept at associating and
  integrating information than conscious processing."
- CRITICAL HONESTY: the authors concede the mechanism is unsettled — "it is not yet clear why incubation is
  helpful," and known moderators "do not really speak to" whether the cause is unconscious processing or
  merely the consequences of distraction (e.g., forgetting fixation).
- This is the CONTESTED interface: the incubation EFFECT is real (Sio & Ormerod), but the strong UNCONSCIOUS
  THOUGHT explanation behind it failed multiple replications (see unconscious-thought-advantage critiques).
- Design implication: build in breaks (the effect is robust), but do NOT market a tool on a specific
  "unconscious processing" mechanism — the safer, better-supported mechanism is set-shifting / fixation
  decay during low-load distraction.
