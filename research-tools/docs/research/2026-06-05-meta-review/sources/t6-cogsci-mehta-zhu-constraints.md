## Key Findings

- A salient sense of resource scarcity activates a "constraint mindset" that reduces functional fixedness
  and increases novelty of product use — limits push people past an object's default function.
- Abundance can have a NEGATIVE effect on creativity: when resources are plentiful, people default to the
  obvious, conventional use.
- Mechanism explicitly links to functional fixedness (Duncker) — constraint is the de-fixation lever.
- Design implication: deliberately imposed constraints (fewer inputs, tighter scope) are a feature, not a
  limitation, for an ideation tool — BUT see contrarian note: this is a JCR consumer-creativity finding,
  not unbounded; extreme scarcity demonstrably harms (the relationship is non-monotonic in the broader lit).
