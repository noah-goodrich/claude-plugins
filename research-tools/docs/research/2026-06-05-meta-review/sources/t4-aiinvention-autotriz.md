## Key Findings

- AutoTRIZ constrains an LLM to a fixed TRIZ knowledge base (39 Engineering Parameters, Contradiction
  Matrix, 40 Inventive Principles) and walks a four-step reasoning flow: identify problem -> abstract to
  contradictions -> retrieve inventive principles -> generate solutions. This is RAG-style grounding of
  invention in distilled patent knowledge.
- On consistency, 7 of 10 textbook cases matched or half-matched the reference TRIZ analysis within the
  top-3 detected contradictions — partial, not high, fidelity.
- The authors concede there is "no objective mechanism to evaluate the effectiveness of generated
  solutions" — users must judge quality themselves; and that LLM outputs "may contain hallucinations."
- Represents a distinct architectural branch: structured/methodology-grounded ideation, as opposed to
  the open-ended generate-debate-evolve loops of Co-Scientist and the wet-lab loops of Coscientist/Robin.

## Verified Quote(s)

**Location reference:** Section 3.2 (knowledge base) and Section 5 "Discussion" (limitations);

> The internal fixed knowledge base includes three main components: (1) the TRIZ 39 Engineering
> Parameters, (2) the TRIZ Contradiction Matrix, (3) the TRIZ 40 Inventive Principles.

> There is no objective mechanism to evaluate the effectiveness of generated solutions. Users must
> independently assess solution quality and rank them for practical use.

> The solutions generated by the LLMs may contain hallucinations or erroneous information.
