## Key Findings

- Practitioner community is GENUINELY SPLIT on whether models converge: some see aggregate
  convergence of general-purpose SFT base models; others insist outputs diverge even with identical
  models and data.
- The thread surfaces the "Platonic Representation Hypothesis" — a formal research idea that models
  trend toward a shared statistical representation of reality — as the academic anchor for the
  convergence intuition.
- Net read: even technically sophisticated users do NOT treat homogenization as settled; the lived
  signal is "directional convergence, but with real residual variability." This tempers the
  stronger academic homogenization claims.
- For an invention engine: the boots-on-ground view supports treating model convergence as a real
  but partial pressure — design for diversity, but don't assume zero residual variance to exploit.
