## Key Findings

- Minority/dissenting viewpoints stimulate DIVERGENT thinking and more creative decisions — even when
  the dissenter is wrong — by forcing reconsideration of alternatives and seeking more information.
- AUTHENTIC dissent beats DEVIL'S ADVOCATE: role-played opposition tends to bolster the original view,
  whereas genuinely-held dissent triggers real divergent search. This is the critical design nuance —
  a tool that fakes a critic may not get the benefit.
- Implication for ideation tooling: surface genuine alternative framings/positions, not a token
  contrarian script.
