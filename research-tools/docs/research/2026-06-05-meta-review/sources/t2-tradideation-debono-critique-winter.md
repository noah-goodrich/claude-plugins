## Key Findings

- de Bono's methods are "wrapped in extravagant claims" with scientific support that "is not very
  robust or credible"; his success examples are anecdotal and unreferenced, hence unverifiable.
- Wikipedia's own Criticism section concedes "sparse research evidence" that Six Thinking Hats
  produces generalised improvements in thinking performance attributable to the training.
- The method's defensible value is as a structured group-facilitation/parallel-thinking ritual,
  not as an empirically validated cognitive intervention.
- This places de Bono firmly on the "folklore" side of the evidence ledger relative to TRIZ
  (case-study evidence) or creativity training broadly (meta-analytic, if deflated, evidence).
