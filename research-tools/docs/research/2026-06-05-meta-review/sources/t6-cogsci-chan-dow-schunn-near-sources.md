## Key Findings

- The popular "far analogies / think outside the box" doctrine is NOT robustly supported. In real platform
  data, conceptually CLOSER sources were associated with MORE creative ideas — an inverse relationship
  between conceptual distance and design creativity, robust across 12 problems.
- Proposed mechanism: creative ideas accumulate from many small insights that "stretch the boundaries just
  a bit at a time," not single distant leaps.
- This directly CONTESTS the crowd/AI analogical-innovation literature (PNAS 2019; Kalogerakis 2010) that
  champions far sources. The honest reading: optimal analogical distance is MEDIUM / contingent, not maximal.
- Design implication: an ideation tool should NOT default to maximally distant prompts; it should tune
  distance and likely favor near-to-medium, problem-relevant sources.
