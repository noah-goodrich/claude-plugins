## Key Findings

- Electronic brainstorming (EBS) "almost always outperform[s] verbal brainstorming groups" — the
  production-blocking penalty of face-to-face brainstorming is medium-specific, not intrinsic to
  the brainstorming concept.
- EBS vs. nominal groups is genuinely mixed: some studies favor EBS, some favor nominal, some find
  no difference (DeRosa et al. 2007 among the no-difference set).
- Group size is the decisive moderator: for large groups (12+), EBS beats nominal; for small
  groups (3–4) the two are roughly equivalent. The classic "individuals beat groups" result is
  thus bounded, not universal.
- This is the institutional counterweight to a naive "brainstorming is dead" reading of Diehl &
  Stroebe: remove production blocking (via electronic/anonymous tools) and scale the group, and
  interacting groups can match or beat pooled individuals.
