## Key Findings

- Nominal groups (individuals working alone, pooled) reliably outperform interacting brainstorming
  groups on BOTH quantity and quality of non-redundant ideas — large effect sizes (r ≈ .56–.57).
- The productivity loss grows as group size grows; bigger interacting groups lose more.
- The result overturns the practitioner intuition that "more heads in a room = more ideas."
- Establishes the empirical baseline that any tool augmenting ideation must beat: parallel
  individual generation, not synchronous group verbal brainstorming.
