# Deep Research → Research (evidence mode)

`/deep-research` is now a **thin alias**. The full citation-synthesis pipeline it used to own —
the novelty probe, tier selection, source discovery and evaluation, the independent Phase 3.5
citation verification, and the executable citation gate — lives unchanged in the unified
**`research`** skill as its **evidence mode**.

**Do this:** invoke the `research-tools:research` skill and proceed in **evidence mode** (the
`## Evidence Mode (Phases 1–6)` section). Pass the user's research question through unchanged.

Nothing about the evidence pipeline changed — only the front door. The citation gate still arms
and fires exactly as before.
