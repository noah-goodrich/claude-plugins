# Brainstorm → Research (decision-design mode)

`/brainstorm` is now a **thin alias**. The design-council pipeline it used to own — parallel
from-zero research tracks, neutral candidate options, the contradiction forge, the five-persona
council, and the blind review of the recommendation — lives in the unified **`research`** skill as
its **decision-design mode**.

**Do this:** invoke the `research-tools:research` skill and proceed in **decision-design mode**
(the `# Decision-Design Mode` section). Pass the user's problem statement through unchanged. If the
decision also rests on a defensible factual evidence base, use **hybrid mode** instead.

Everything you expect from `/brainstorm` is preserved there — only the front door changed.
